# Full-history EDA and baselines (run 2026-10-08)

Data: 32,445 hourly snapshots, 2023-01-01 to 2026-09-30, 18 hospitals, 584,010 hospital-hour rows. 0 parse failures.
Old format (categorical `topWait`) = 433,134 rows; new format (per-triage p50/p95) = 150,876 rows (from 2025-10-13).
AQHI matched for 97.9% of rows; HKO daily weather for 97.8% (missing = Sep 2026, HKO lag). 12,942 public-holiday hours.

## What the data shows
- Strong daily cycle (cat 4/5 wait, new format): trough 08:00 = 91 min, peak 23:00 = 229 min.
- Share of hours with cat 4/5 wait > 2 h: 74.8% in 2023-2025 (old format) vs 54.8% since Oct 2025 (new format).
  Part of this gap is a convention change (old = lower bound of a category), part may be real. Treat the two eras separately.
- Busiest A&Es (mean cat 4/5 wait): Kwong Wah 255 min, United Christian 253, Tuen Mun 252, Prince of Wales 247, PYNEH 243.
- Cat 3 median wait moves little (MAE of naive baselines 6-7 min), so cat 4/5 is the right regression target.

## Baselines to beat (test = Sep 2026, train = everything before)
Regression, cat 4/5 median wait in minutes (new format only):

| horizon | persistence MAE | historical-mean MAE |
|---|---|---|
| 1 h  | 35.6 | 62.3 |
| 3 h  | 62.0 | 51.6 |
| 24 h | 61.9 | 51.1 |

Classification, wait > 2 h (both formats), base rate 52.9% in test:

| horizon | persistence acc | hist-mean acc | hist-mean recall / precision | AUC (pers / hist) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 h  | 0.729 | 0.618 | 0.955 / 0.585 | n/a |
| 24 h | 0.734 | 0.617 | 0.956 / 0.584 | 0.733 / 0.747 |

Reading: at 1 h ahead persistence is nearly unbeatable; at 3 h and 24 h a hospital x weekday x hour mean already wins on MAE
(51 min). The historical-mean classifier over-predicts "> 2 h" because its training years (old format) had a 75% base rate
versus 53% now: a regime shift the models must handle (era feature, or train on new format only, or recalibrate the threshold).
