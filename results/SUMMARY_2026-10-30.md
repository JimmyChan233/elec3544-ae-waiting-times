# First models vs baselines (run 2026-10-30)

Data: hourly table rebuilt with October 2026 (593,388 hospital-hours, 2023-01-01 .. 2026-10-29) and the 2023-2024 holiday fix.
Split by target time: train < 2026-09-01 · validation = Sep 2026 (all tuning) · test = Oct 2026 (new data, never used for choices).
October archive has 7 empty days (5, 7, 8, 12, 16, 25, 26) → test = 9,378 hospital-hours. HKO has not published October weather.
Code: `src/train_models.py` (v1 = all features, v2 = `--drop weather air`), figures from `src/plot_results.py`.

## Headline (v2, test month = October 2026)

| | 3 h ahead | 24 h ahead |
|---|---|---|
| Regression MAE, hist-mean baseline | 52.0 min | 52.0 min |
| Regression MAE, best model (grad boosting) | **41.1 min (−21%)** | 52.4 min (no gain) |
| > 2 h classifier AUC, hist-mean → grad boosting | 0.836 → **0.892** | 0.836 → 0.838 |
| > 2 h classifier accuracy, hist-mean → grad boosting | 0.754 → **0.804** | 0.754 → 0.759 |

- **3 h: clear win.** Every learned model beats both baselines; ordering on test = OLS/ridge/lasso 44.8 < random forest 42.8 < gradient boosting 41.1.
  Ridge/lasso give the same MAE as OLS (alpha barely matters with 138k rows) — regularisation is not where the gain is.
- **24 h: no reliable gain.** Validation improved (48.7 vs 51.5) but the test month did not (52.4 vs 52.0). With these features,
  a day ahead the hospital × weekday × hour average is about as good as it gets. This is the main open problem.
- **Feature groups (drop one group, refit, val MAE at 3 h):** recent history +9.7 min, calendar +1.6, hospital +1.4,
  weather −0.3 and AQHI −0.1 (removing them helps). At 24 h weather removal helped by 1.4 min → dropped in v2.
  Answer to the proposal's secondary question so far: congestion is driven by its own momentum, not by weather or air quality.
- **Regime shift handled.** The 10-08 hist-mean classifier over-predicted "> 2 h" (acc 0.62) because it averaged the old
  feed (70% positive). Computing the baseline on the current format only lifts it to acc 0.75; for the models,
  an era flag (regime A, all 571k rows) and new-format-only training (regime B, 138k rows) end up equal (AUC 0.892 vs 0.893 at 3 h).
- **Error analysis (3 h, grad boosting):** error is largest at night (MAE ≈ 60 min at 00–02, ≈ 20 min at 07–08), tracking the
  level of the wait. Biggest gains over the baseline at the busiest, most volatile A&Es (PYNEH −30 min, North District −23,
  Tuen Mun −22); almost none at stable ones (Yan Chai, North Lantau, St John ≈ −1).
